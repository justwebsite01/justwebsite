How should I use a legal guide or calculator?

Use a guide to identify questions and organise documents, not to decide a legal outcome. Calculators are limited by their assumptions and the information entered; their output is not a valuation, legal opinion or guarantee. For a notice, deadline, transaction or dispute, seek advice based on the complete record.
